# Design System: Podcast Studio

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Calm Control Room"**

Podcast Studio is the category standard played straight: the familiar, polished creator-tool layout (a dark sidebar beside a light workspace, a segmented step bar, a sticky player) held to Riverside's level of finish. Nothing in it is decorative. The pipeline and the audio are the content, and the interface's job is to make their state readable at a glance by someone who started a job, left, and came back. Precision, state colour and motion carry the identity; there is no motif, texture, illustration or quirk.

Density is that of a working tool, not a landing page: 15px body type, 36px controls, hairline-bordered white panels on a cool grey ground, and a narrow type ramp (11px to 26px) whose hierarchy comes mostly from weight. Violet appears only where something is primary, current or playing. Each speaker owns one channel colour across every screen, so who says what reads before a word is read. Indian scripts are first-class: one font stack renders Latin and nine Indic scripts in the same line, so code-mixed speech reads as one voice.

Both themes are full citizens. Dark is not an inversion: the violet splits into a lighter text tone and a deeper fill, state and speaker colours brighten to hold contrast on near-black, and shadows turn black and heavier.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Dark 240px sidebar with running jobs pinned to its foot; light workspace; app-style bottom tab bar on phones.
- One violet accent, three state colours, five speaker channels; everything else is neutral.
- Inter plus Noto Sans per Indian script in a single stack, tabular numerals for every time and count.
- Flat hairline panels; shadows only for lift, stickiness and floating layers.
- Motion that reports state: page axis, cover-to-episode morph, live progress, arriving items, a transcript that follows playback.

### Motion

**The Motion Shows State Rule.** Every animation answers "what changed?": a page moved, a job advanced, an item arrived, a line is playing, a press registered. Nothing moves for ambience.

- **Easing.** Settle with `--ease` (`cubic-bezier(.2, .8, .2, 1)`) for almost every state change; leave with `--ease-in` (`cubic-bezier(.4, 0, 1, 1)`). Phone pager slides use `cubic-bezier(.2, 0, 0, 1)`; the cover morph uses `cubic-bezier(.3, 0, .2, 1)`.
- **Durations.** Hover and colour changes 150ms; tab, step and chapter changes 200ms; the playing transcript row's background 250ms; press feedback 100ms (`scale(.97)` buttons, `.98` language tiles, `.92` play buttons); progress and speaker-share bars grow over 600ms.
- **Page transitions** (View Transitions API, `swap()` in app.js): on phones tabs slide a full viewport width in 200ms like a pager, direction set by tab order; on desktop the page moves 28px along the same axis and cross-fades (out 140ms ease-in, in 260ms after a 60ms delay) while the sidebar, named `tabbar`, stays put. Opening an episode grows the clicked cover into the page (420ms, 12px corners, float shadow; old content fades out in 160ms, the new page fades in over 260ms after 140ms); closing reverses into the same cover.
- **Arrivals.** Facts, script lines, log entries and sample lines that appear during a running job rise 6px and fade in over 350ms; items present on first paint do not animate. The job pill rises 4px in 250ms. Count badges and step check marks pop from `scale(.4)` in 300ms.
- **Running.** The running dot pings an expanding amber ring every 1.4s. A step's progress bar fills to its known fraction; when the fraction is unknowable it runs an indeterminate 35%-wide sweep every 1.3s.
- **Loading.** Skeleton bars share one page-fixed highlight that sweeps every 1.6s, kept in phase across re-renders.
- **Listening.** The transcript scrolls the playing line to the centre only when it has left the band between the sticky player and the tab bar; the chapter strip scrolls its current chip into view; play and pause glyphs cross-scale (150ms opacity, 200ms scale).
- **Toasts** slide up 10px and fade in (200ms opacity, 250ms transform) and leave the same way.
- **Reduced motion.** All keyframe animations stop; the indeterminate bar becomes a static full-width bar at 35% opacity; every transition duration drops to 0; page transitions are skipped entirely; transcript following jumps instead of scrolling smoothly.

## Colors

A cool neutral workspace with one violet voice, three state colours, five speaker channels and a deep cover palette; every hue has a job.

### Primary
- **Studio Violet** (#5B3DF5; `--accent` and `--accent-strong`): the primary button, the main play button and any line that is playing, the count badge, the current chapter chip, the current phone tab, selected language tiles, tags, the focus ring, caret, range and checkbox accents. In dark it splits: **Violet Text** (#A396FF, `--accent`) for violet text, rings and marks, **Violet Fill** (#6A4FFF, `--accent-strong`) for filled surfaces that carry white text.
- **Violet Pressed** (#4E34D3; dark #5B44DB): hover on filled violet, mixed as 86% fill with black.
- **Violet Wash** (#F1EEFF; dark rgba(139, 123, 255, .14)): selected backgrounds (current chapter, selected language, phone tab pill, tags, fact counts) and the 3px focus halo on fields.
- **Violet Line** (#D9D1FF; dark rgba(139, 123, 255, .38)): borders of violet-washed chips, text selection, hover border on language tiles.
- **Playing Row** (#E3E1F9; dark #201F2F): the transcript line under the playhead, mixed as 12% accent into the ground.

### Secondary
- **Speaker channels** (`--ch1`..`--ch5`, assigned by the speaker's position in the plan, wrapping after five): **Speaker Blue** (#2563EB; dark #6EA8FE), **Speaker Pink** (#C2185B; dark #F472B6), **Speaker Amber** (#B45309; dark #FBBF24), **Speaker Green** (#047857; dark #34D399), **Speaker Teal** (#0E7490; dark #22D3EE). Skeletons for speaker content tint with the channel at 22% (sheen 34%) over the panel. - **Hairline** (#E3E6EB; dark #262A33): panel borders, row dividers, the tab bar's top edge.
- **Control Edge** (#CDD2DA; dark #363B47): borders of buttons and fields, scrollbar thumbs, unplayed scrubber segments (mixed 75% into the panel).
- **Ink** (#14171F; dark #ECEDF1): text; also the toast background.
- **Slate Muted** (#5B6270; dark #9BA2AF): hints, meta, timestamps, quiet buttons, placeholders, idle tab labels.
- **Sidebar Night** (#16181D; dark #0A0B0E), **Sidebar Raised** (#23262E; dark #1B1E25), **Sidebar Ink** (#F2F3F5), **Sidebar Muted** (#AEB4C0; dark #9BA2AF): the desktop sidebar ground, its current and hovered item and job cards, and its two text levels. The sidebar stays dark in the light theme.
- **Skeleton** (#ECEEF2; dark #1C1F26) and **Skeleton Sheen** (#F7F8FA; dark #262A33): loading bars and their sweeping highlight.

### Named Rules
**The One Violet Rule.** Violet marks only what is primary, current or playing. Progress is amber, completion is green, failure is red; violet never stands in for state and never decorates.

**The Speaker Channel Rule.** A speaker's colour is theirs on every screen, assigned by plan order through `--ch1`..`--ch5`. It colours the speaker's name, mode label, bullet and share bar (and the skeletons that stand in for them), never a line's text or a panel.

**The Both Themes Rule.** Every colour token is defined three times: on `:root`, in the `prefers-color-scheme: dark` block guarded by `:root:not([data-theme="light"])`, and under `:root[data-theme="dark"]`. A token missing from either dark block is a bug.

## Typography

**Display Font:** none; the largest type is the 26px page title.
**Body Font:** Inter (variable, optical size 14–32, weights 400–700), followed in one stack by Noto Sans Devanagari, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Oriya and Gurmukhi, then system-ui.
**Label/Mono Font:** none distinct; labels are Inter at intermediate weights, numbers use tabular figures.

**Character:** Inter is the category-standard face, set with `cv11` (single-storey a) and antialiased; the Noto families pick up every Indian script inside the same line, so a Hindi sentence with English words reads as one voice at matching weight.

### Hierarchy
- **Headline** (700, 26px, 1.2, -0.02em; 23px under 760px): the page or episode title, balanced wrapping. A title containing any non-Latin character switches to **Headline Native** (0 tracking, 1.35 line height) so matras and conjuncts are not clipped.
- **Cover Title** (650, 19px, 1.25, -0.01em; 16px on phones): the title printed on an episode cover, clamped to four lines (three on phones).
- **Title** (650, 17px, -0.01em): section headings; the New tab's topic field also sets at 17px/1.45.
- **Subtitle** (600, 15px): chapter and minor headings; card titles under covers (600, 15px, 1.35, two lines).
- **Body** (400, 15px, 1.55): everything by default. Long prose (hints, empty states, ledes) holds to 62–72ch.
- **Script Line** (400, 15.5px, 1.6): editable script lines; audio transcript lines use 15px/1.6; backchannel fillers drop to 14px muted.
- **Numeral** (650, 20px, 1.2, tabular): script stats and the speaker-count stepper.
- **Label** (550, 14px): buttons, segmented options, step names (600); sidebar items 14px/500, current 600; phone tab labels 12px/600.
- **Caption** (400, 13px): hints, field labels (13px muted, label line 550 ink), activity log, meta rows (13.5px).
- **Micro** (600, 12px): fact counts, tags (550), chip numbers, speaker names on lines (650, 13px); count badges and the "Playing" label are 11px at 650 and 600.

### Named Rules
**The Weight Before Size Rule.** The ramp spans 11–26px; hierarchy comes from the weight steps 400, 500, 550, 600, 650 and 700. Add a weight step before adding a size.

**The One Stack Rule.** All text, Latin or Indic, uses the single `--ui` stack, and user content carries its `lang` attribute. Never set an Indian script in a separate family or a fallback-only face.

**The Tabular Numbers Rule.** Times, durations, counts and stats use tabular numerals (`.num`, `time`, stat figures, chip numbers) so live values do not jitter as they change.

## Layout

The app shell switches at 900px. At 900px and wider a fixed 240px dark sidebar holds the lockup, four 40px navigation rows and, pushed to its foot, cards for running jobs; the body is offset by its width. Below 900px a 52px top bar carries the lockup and a fixed bottom tab bar holds four centred tabs (up to 110px each, 52px tall, safe-area aware); the body pads its bottom to clear it.

Content sits in one centred column: 960px wide for the New, Episodes, Voices and Settings tabs, 1360px for an episode. Page padding is 32px top, 40px sides, 48px bottom; under 760px it is 20px, 16px, 24px. An episode stacks a header (56px back chevron, 56px cover thumbnail, title and meta; the job pill and quiet actions on the right), the four-column step bar (columns at least 130px; two by two under 760px), then a work grid of a fluid stage and a 300px sticky activity column 32px apart. Under 1180px the activity column folds beneath the stage behind a hairline, capped at 320px tall.

Collections use auto-fill grids: covers at 196px minimum with 28px row and 20px column gaps (150px, 22/14 on phones), voices 220–230px, persona strips 240px, language tiles 118px. Script lines are a 156px speaker column, the text and hover-revealed actions; transcript lines are a 34px play column, a 124px speaker column, the text and a New take action. On phones both collapse so the text takes a full row under the speaker.

Spacing follows a 4px rhythm with 2px half-steps for tight pairs: 24px between stage sections, 16px between stacked panels and inside panels, 10–12px between list cards, 6–8px inside controls.

### Named Rules
**The Sticky Pair Rule.** On Audio, the player sticks 12px from the top (8px on phones) with a ground-coloured strip above it, and the transcript scrolls under it; the activity column sticks 24px from the top with a fading heading. Apart from the fixed navigation shell, nothing else sticks.

## Elevation & Depth

Hybrid and mostly flat. Panels, cards, list rows and fields sit flat on the ground with a 1px hairline; depth is spent on state. A selected option rises out of its sunken track, a hovered cover lifts, the player floats while the lines pass under it, and only transient layers (toast, the cover morph) cast a large shadow. Shadows are soft and offset downward, cool-tinted (rgba of #101828) in light and black and denser in dark.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Rest lift** (`--shadow-1`: `0 1px 2px rgba(16, 24, 40, .06), 0 1px 3px rgba(16, 24, 40, .08)`; dark `0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, .4)`): episode covers and the header thumbnail, the selected segmented option, the current step tab (with an inset 1px control-edge ring), the scrubber thumb.
- **Hover and sticky** (`--shadow-2`: `0 2px 4px rgba(16, 24, 40, .06), 0 8px 20px rgba(16, 24, 40, .08)`; dark `0 2px 6px rgba(0, 0, 0, .4), 0 10px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, .35)`): a hovered cover (lifted 3px) and the sticky player.
- **Float** (`--shadow-3`: `0 12px 32px rgba(16, 24, 40, .18)`; dark `0 16px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, .55)`): the toast and the card that carries the cover-to-episode morph.
- **Button hairline** (`0 1px 1px rgba(16, 24, 40, .04)`): default buttons at rest.
- **Violet glow** (`0 1px 2px` at 30% and `0 2px 6px` at 35% of `--accent-strong`, mixed with transparent, on the primary button and the main play button): filled violet controls, in each theme's own violet.
- **Focus halo** (`0 0 0 3px` Violet Wash on fields; `0 0 0 3px` Violet Line on the scrubber thumb): keyboard and input focus, alongside a 2px violet outline offset 2px on everything else.

### Named Rules
**The Flat Panel Rule.** A resting panel gets a hairline, never a shadow. Shadow means raised by state, stuck in place, or floating; the one exception is the episode cover (and its header thumbnail), which rests at Rest lift as an object to pick up and open.

## Shapes

Gently rounded, never pill-shaped by default. Controls take 8px corners, tiles and rows 10px, panels and covers 12px, the player 14px. Pills exist only where the element is a status, a chip or a bar, and take the pill radius (999px): the job pill, chapter chips, the count badge, the Playing tag, the phone tab pill, progress bars and scrubber segments. Tags keep 10px. Circles are reserved for play buttons (32px line, 44px main), 8px status dots, 6px speaker bullets and the step check. Loading bars use 4px corners; covers are exact squares.

Borders are 1px: Hairline around panels, Control Edge around controls. Selection is drawn as a violet border plus a 1px inset violet ring (language tiles) or a lift out of a track (segmented options, step tabs). Icons are inline SVG line drawings with 1.8px round-capped strokes: 16px in buttons, 20px in navigation, a 34px chevron at 2.2px for Back. Generated covers carry the only graphic device: per-speaker rows of 7px rounded "speech blocks" in the cover's text colour at 32% opacity.

### Named Rules
**The Concentric Corner Rule.** A shape nested in a track is rounded tighter than the track by about its inset: segmented options 7px inside a 10px track with 3px padding, step tabs 9px inside a 12px bar with 4px padding.

## Components

### Buttons
Precise and quiet; colour is earned.
- **Shape:** gently curved (8px), 36px tall, 14px side padding, 14px/550 label, 6px gap to a 16px icon.
- **Primary:** Studio Violet fill (`--accent-strong`) with white text and a violet glow; one per area: Make the whole episode, Save plan, Run research, Write the script, Generate audio, Save settings.
- **Secondary:** Panel White with a Control Edge border and the button hairline; for the alternative path (Plan first, Show more).
- **Quiet:** transparent, muted text, 10px side padding; header actions (Duplicate, Delete, Remove cover), per-line tools (New take, insert), downloads (MP3, WAV, script.txt).
- **Hover / Focus / Active:** hover moves to Sunken Track (filled violet darkens to Violet Pressed); Delete washes Stopped Red on hover; press scales to .97 in 100ms; focus shows the 2px violet ring; disabled drops to 45% opacity.
- **Play buttons:** circular, Panel White with a Control Edge border; hover turns border and glyph violet; the playing button fills violet and its play glyph cross-scales into pause. The main play button is 44px violet with glow.

### Chips
- **Chapter chips:** 32px pills on Panel White with a Hairline border: bold muted number, title (max 24ch, ellipsis), tabular time. Current chapter: Violet Wash fill, Violet Line border, violet text. They scroll horizontally in one row.
- **Tags and counts:** Violet Wash with violet 12px text ("female default", "4 facts"); an empty count falls back to Sunken Track and muted.
- **Count badge:** an 18px violet pill with an 11px white number, on the Episodes tab for running jobs; it pops in.
- **Playing label:** an 18px violet pill reading "Playing" after the speaker name of the current transcript line.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 12px (`panel`); the player 14px.
- **Background:** Panel White on the Workspace ground.
- **Shadow Strategy:** flat (see The Flat Panel Rule); covers rest at Rest lift, the player at Hover and sticky.
- **Border:** 1px Hairline.
- **Internal Padding:** 20px 24px 22px for form panels; 14–18px for list cards (questions, persona strips, outline segments, voices); 16px 20px for script sheets; 28px for empty states.
- **Number chips:** segment and chapter numbers sit in 24–28px Sunken Track squares with 7–8px corners, muted 650 weight.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** 38px tall, 7px 11px padding, Panel White, 1px Control Edge, 8px corners; hover darkens the edge toward muted; placeholders in Slate Muted. Labels stack above in 13px with a 550 ink label line.
- **Focus:** border turns violet with a 3px Violet Wash halo; no outline.
- **In-place editing:** script lines and speaker or mode selectors are borderless and transparent until hovered (Hairline or Control Edge appears); a focused line gets a Panel White background. Textareas grow with their content.
- **Segmented control:** options in a Sunken Track with 3px padding; the selected option lifts to Panel White with Rest lift. Used for code-mix level, episode state filter and theme.
- **Language tiles:** 54px tiles with the native name at 16px/550 and the English name in 12px muted; selected gets a violet border, inset ring and Violet Wash, its small text turning violet.
- **Stepper:** 36px square minus and plus buttons around a borderless 20px/650 tabular number.
- **Checkbox and range:** native controls tinted with `accent-color`.

### Navigation
- **Sidebar (≥900px):** Sidebar Night ground; items 40px tall with an 11px icon gap at 14px/500 in Sidebar Muted; hover and current both take Sidebar Raised with Sidebar Ink text, current at 600 with a white icon. The running-job count badge sits right-aligned in the Episodes row. A 12px/600 muted "In progress" heading tops the job cards: Sidebar Raised, 10px corners, two-line title, amber dot and task, and a 3px progress bar.
- **Tab bar (<900px):** Panel White with a Hairline top edge; icon over a 12px/600 label; the current tab's icon sits in a 56×28 Violet Wash pill turning violet, its label ink.
- **Step bar:** four equal tabs in a Sunken Track; each shows a numbered ring or a green check, a step name and a small note (segments, facts, minutes). The current step is raised (Panel White, Rest lift, inset edge ring); done and live steps read in ink; a live step carries an amber 3px progress bar along its foot.

### Job Pill and Progress
The episode's job status. Running: Running Wash pill with amber text and a pinging amber dot ("Writing the script…"). Stopped: Stopped Wash with a red dot and the first 90 characters of the error. Progress bars are 3px amber on an 18% amber track, determinate where the fraction is known (research questions answered, segments written, lines voiced) and indeterminate otherwise; both widths ease over 600ms.

### Episode Cover
A square card that identifies an episode at a glance. With no uploaded image it is filled from the cover palette by id hash: native language name and speaker count across the top (the count hides on phones), one row of speech blocks per speaker, and the title at Cover Title size. An uploaded image fills the square edge to edge, the top labels sit on 66%-black 6px-cornered plates, and the title moves beneath the image. Under every cover: the title (15px/600, turns violet on hover) and a state line, which is amber with a pinging dot while a job runs. Hover lifts the cover 3px to Hover and sticky; the cover is also the shared element that morphs into the episode page.

### Player and Followed Transcript
The Audio step's centre. A sticky 14px-cornered panel: the 44px violet play button, the current chapter title and "Line n, SPEAKER" (or a pace summary when idle), quiet MP3/WAV downloads; then a scrubber cut into one segment per chapter (played portion violet at 55% into the panel, the current segment full violet, a 14px white thumb with a violet ring) between tabular times; then the chapter chips. Below, transcript lines grouped by chapter: a play button, line number, speaker name in its channel colour with any mode note (filler, cuts in), and the text as a button that seeks to that line. The playing line takes the Playing Row tint and the "Playing" label; its play button shows pause. Backchannel fillers are indented 28px and take their speaker's colour on the mode label.

### Activity Log
A sticky 300px column of agent events, newest last: agent name at 600, a muted tabular timestamp, then the event; thought traces in muted, errors in Stopped Red. Its heading masks the list with the ground colour and a 10px fade.

### Loading Placeholders
Skeleton bars shaped like the text they stand in for: each as tall as 0.72em of that line, vertically centred in the line box, with widths from a fixed list so shapes hold across re-renders. One sheen, fixed to the page and 250vw wide, sweeps every skeleton in step. Skeletons for buttons, covers, thumbnails, dots and the player match those components' sizes and corners; speaker-specific skeletons tint with the channel colour.

### Toast
Bottom centre above the tab bar on phones, bottom right (24px) on desktop; inverse colours (Ink ground, Workspace text), 10px corners, Float shadow, 14px/500. Errors use Stopped Red with white text and stay 8 seconds instead of 3.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** fill violet surfaces with `--accent-strong` and colour violet text and marks with `--accent`; they are the same #5B3DF5 in light and split to #6A4FFF and #A396FF in dark.
- **Do** assign speaker colour with `color(i)` (plan order, wrapping after five) and set it as `--c` on the row, so names, bullets, selectors and skeleton tints all follow it.
- **Do** use amber for running, green for done and red for stopped, each with its wash for pills and boxes.
- **Do** set user content with its `lang` attribute and switch a non-Latin page title to the native headline (0 tracking, 1.35 line height).
- **Do** use tabular numerals for every time, duration, count and stat.
- **Do** give panels a 1px Hairline and no shadow; raise only covers, and what is selected, hovered, sticky or floating.
- **Do** keep controls at 8px corners, rows and tiles at 10px, panels and covers at 12px, the player at 14px, pills at the pill radius.
- **Do** show a running step's real fraction when it is known and the indeterminate bar when it is not.
- **Do** animate arrivals only for items that appear while a job runs, never on a page's first paint.
- **Do** add every new keyframe animation to the `prefers-reduced-motion` block and every new colour token to both dark blocks.
- **Do** build loading states as skeletons in the shape of the content, on the shared page-fixed sheen.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use violet for progress, success, decoration or emphasis that is not primary, current or playing.
- **Don't** add a second accent hue; the speaker channels and cover palette are identity colours, not accents.
- **Don't** colour a line's text, a card or a panel with its speaker colour; the colour belongs on the name, mode label, bullet and share bar.
- **Don't** set Indian scripts in a different family from Latin text or rely on system fallback for them.
- **Don't** add a motif, texture, illustration or quirk to the workspace; the product's look is the category standard played straight.
- **Don't** animate anything that does not report a change of state, and don't let a motion run without a reduced-motion fallback.
- **Don't** make a new element sticky beyond the player and the activity column.
